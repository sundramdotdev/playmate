## 🔍 SEO & App Store Optimization (ASO) Strategy

1. **Title Keyword Placement:** Putting `Offline Game Tools` in the Android title directly targets high-volume non-branded intent ("offline game tools", "offline dice").
2. **First 3 Sentences:** The first 167 characters of the Google Play description clearly highlight offline capability, zero ads, and board game utility, directly maximizing search crawler indexing.
3. **Bullet Structure:** Organized with visual emoji bullets to maximize reading comprehension and store conversion rates (CVR).
