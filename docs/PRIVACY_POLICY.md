# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 1, 2026

**PlayMate** ("we", "our", or "the Application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, storage, usage, and your rights when using the PlayMate mobile application on Android and iOS devices.

---

## 1. Core Principle: Privacy-by-Default & Offline-First

PlayMate is architected as an **offline-first utility**. You do not need to create an account, provide an email address, or maintain an active internet connection to access any of PlayMate's core tools. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal data to any third parties under any circumstances.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Data Stored Exclusively on Your Device (Local Storage)
All gameplay information created during your sessions is saved exclusively in your device's sandboxed local storage using Hive:
- Player names entered into the Team Generator or Score Tracker.
- Match scores, cricket overs, run tallies, and tournament brackets.
- User settings (Dark/Light mode preference, sound/haptic toggles, shake sensitivity).

**This data never leaves your device** unless you explicitly share it via native OS sharing features or initiate a manual backup.

### B. Aggregated & Anonymized Telemetry (Optional)
If connected to the internet, PlayMate may transmit non-personally identifiable diagnostic and analytical telemetry via Google Firebase:
- **Device & System Data:** Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, locale, and memory capacity.
- **Usage Metrics:** Aggregated counters of feature interactions (e.g., number of times a D6 die was rolled, coin flips performed, tournament bracket generated).
- **Diagnostics & Crash Logs:** Anonymized stack traces through Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix software bugs.

We **do not** collect:
- Names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
- Precise GPS location coordinates.
- Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone feeds.
- Advertising Identifiers (IDFA on iOS or GAID on Android for ad tracking).

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

PlayMate is designed for users of all ages, including families and children playing board games. 
- We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
- Because PlayMate does not require user registration and collects zero PII, the application complies with the **Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** requirements regarding minor privacy.

---

## 5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Under applicable data protection regulations (including **GDPR**, **CCPA**, and **CalOPPA**), you maintain the following rights:

1. **Right to Access & Portability:** You have full access to your game records inside the app's history screens.
2. **Right to Erasure (Data Deletion):** You can permanently erase all locally stored matches, scores, and statistics at any time by navigating to:
   $$\text{Settings} \longrightarrow \text{Data Management} \longrightarrow \text{Clear All Data}$$
   Uninstalling the application from your device will also purge all local Hive database files immediately.
3. **Right to Opt-Out of Telemetry:** You can disable diagnostic telemetry within the app settings.

## 6. Regulatory Compliance

### General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), our lawful basis for processing aggregated diagnostic data is legitimate interest in maintaining application stability and security.

### California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. California consumers have the right to request deletion of any telemetry data collected.

### Google Play User Data Policy & Apple App Store Guidelines
PlayMate adheres strictly to the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and Apple App Store Review Guidelines regarding User Data, Data Minimization, and App Privacy Nutrition Labels.
